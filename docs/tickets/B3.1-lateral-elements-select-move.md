# B3.1 — Lateral Elements, select, move Shear Lines

- **Track:** B (Aidan)
- **Phase:** B3 (1:15–2:00)
- **Type:** AFK
- **Blocked by:** B1.1

## What to build

1. **Element tool:**
   - Press on a Shear Line and drag along it to create a `LateralElement` (start < end, snapped to 1 ft).
   - The element is clamped to the line's length from `crossSection`.
   - A kind toggle (shear wall / braced frame) sets `kind`.
2. **Select tool** sets `selection` and highlights the selected item:
   - Click a Shear Line → `{ kind: 'shearLine' }`
   - Click a Lateral Element → `{ kind: 'lateralElement' }`
   - Click an Opening → `{ kind: 'opening' }`
3. **Move a Shear Line:** drag a selected line perpendicular to itself, snapping to 1 ft, or to Gridlines in Grid mode.
   - Its name stays the same. If it lands on a Gridline, it takes that Gridline's name.
   - Results update live while dragging.
4. **Delete tool:** click a Shear Line, Lateral Element, or Opening to remove it.
