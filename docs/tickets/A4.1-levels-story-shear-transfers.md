# A4.1 — Multiple Levels: story shear, Transfer Forces, transfer Collectors

- **Track:** A (Hamid)
- **Phase:** A4 (2:00–2:45)
- **Type:** AFK
- **Blocked by:** A3.1

## What to build

1. **`engine/levels.ts`:** process Levels **top to bottom** ([spec §5.8](../spec.md#58-lateral-elements-and-story-shear)).
   - A line continues into the Level below when it has the same direction and its position is within 0.5 ft.
   - `storyShear` = own reactionFx + the storyShear of continuing lines above.
   - Set `continuesBelow`.
   - Lines on the bottom Level always continue.
2. **Transfer Forces** ([spec §5.10](../spec.md#510-transfer-forces)):
   - For a line that doesn't continue, P = its storyShear.
   - Apply P as a point load at its position on the Level below, in **both** the F_x and F_px runs.
   - Add a `TransferResult` to that Level's `transfersIn`.
   - **Transfer Collector:** v_in over the stopped line's (clipped) elements, v_out uniform over the net length. `collectorPlf` = P / net length × 1000.
   - Raise `transfer-outside` (error) and skip the transfer if the position misses the Diaphragm below.
3. **Lateral Elements** now use the accumulated storyShear.
4. **Results panel:**
   - Shear Lines table: add story shear and "continues?" columns.
   - **Transfers** table: from Level/line, position, force, plf, Collector max.
